License: Pointer to cursor/plugins at the pinned commit. Local summary, not a vendored copy.

## Method

1. Open with the task the reader is trying to finish.
2. Put prerequisites and the exact command before the explanation.
3. Say what success looks like and what a common failure looks like.
4. Do not document a flag that was not verified.
5. Keep this summary local. Do not copy upstream plugin text wholesale.
