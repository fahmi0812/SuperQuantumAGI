## Method

1. Use only experience the candidate actually has. Do not invent employers, degrees, or metrics.
2. Mirror the role's real requirements. Cut irrelevant history instead of padding.
3. For interviews, answer in the candidate's voice and include a concrete example.
4. Do not scrape or store private job-board credentials.
5. Do not invent employers, degrees, or metrics.
