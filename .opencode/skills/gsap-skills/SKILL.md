## Method

1. Put motion on a timeline so it can be paused, reversed, and tested.
2. Honor prefers-reduced-motion.
3. Do not animate layout properties when transform and opacity are enough.
4. Keep GSAP plugin files out of this repo until their license is confirmed for vendoring.
5. Do not vendor GSAP plugin binaries.
